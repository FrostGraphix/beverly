# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations, finance, support, and field teams use this workspace to run Beverly CRM workflows, monitor wallet/vending activity, review vendor activity, and make release or operational decisions from live system data.

## Product Purpose

The product provides a controlled CRM and wallet operations surface for managing vendors, customers, vending activity, funding queues, receipts, reports, and administrative checks. Success means users can see status clearly, act on exceptions quickly, and trust the data before production release.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. The interface should feel operationally mature, readable under pressure, and restrained enough for repeated daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card mosaics, decorative dashboards that hide task priority, low-contrast gray text, ornamental motion, and marketing-style copy inside operational screens.

## Design Principles

- Put live status and required action ahead of decoration.
- Preserve trust through clear hierarchy, explicit state labels, and stable controls.
- Keep dense information scannable without making the surface feel cramped.
- Use motion only to clarify state changes or confirm interaction.
- Treat accessibility and release readiness as part of the design, not a final pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, readable data labels, and touch targets suitable for tablet use in field and office contexts.
